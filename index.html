FAQ

Common questions

Does this work with the Hallowed Concepts mod? +
Yes — the trainer is built specifically around the Hallowed Concepts survivor and skill reworks. It also runs fine on vanilla Risk of Rain 2, but the survivor unlocker and item spawner are tuned for modded runs.
Will it get me banned in multiplayer? +
Risk of Rain 2 has no anti-cheat and no bans. Still, use it offline or in private lobbies with friends — other players in public matches will see your modified damage, cooldowns and inventory.
Where does it save my lunar coins? +
The lunar coin editor writes directly to your profile's save data. The trainer makes a backup of the original file before the first edit, so you can restore your pre-edit balance at any time.
Is it really free and open source? +
Completely. The full source is published on GitHub, there is no paywall, no key system and no bundled adware. Download the build or compile it yourself — both links point to the same repository.
